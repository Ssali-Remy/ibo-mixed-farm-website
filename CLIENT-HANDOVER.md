- **All seven pages are built and working**, on both phones and computers.

  sessions — using the photos you sent through.

  descriptions and structure are all in place for that.

## What we still need from you

A few photographs and a few pieces of information are still missing. None of
these stop the site from working — they just leave a handful of gaps we'd
like to close.

### Photographs

1. **A photo of each of you individually** — you, Betty, and Olla. We were
   sent several group photos, but nobody in them is identified by name, so we
   can't safely put the wrong face next to the wrong name. One clear photo of
   each person, with the name confirmed, and we can finish your team section.
2. **A photo of a plant clinic or disease-diagnosis session** — someone
   examining a sick plant.
3. **A photo of soil sampling or soil testing** being carried out.
4. **A sketch, plan, or an aerial/drone photo** showing how the farm's
   different blocks are laid out.
5. **A photo of any value-addition or processing work** — for example,
   packaging produce or bottling honey.

### Information

- **A working phone number** for the farm. This is the single most important
  item — it's needed for the WhatsApp button, and to get your farm verified
  on Google.
- **Any award, certificate, or press mention** from the last year or so that
  you'd like us to highlight — on the website and in the social media posts
  we're planning.
- **A couple of short customer testimonials or success stories**, if you have
  any you're happy to share.
- **The exact dates** for the milestones on your farm's timeline (we have the
  order of events, just not the years).
- **Scans of your accreditation and registration certificates**, for the
  credentials section.

Sending these through — even a little at a time — lets us finish the last
few sections properly.
