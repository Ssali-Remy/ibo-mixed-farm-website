Finding us

Bwizibwera, Mbarara.

The farm sits in Rwanyamahembe sub-county, on the Bwizibwera side of Mbarara City. Tell us when you are setting off and we will guide you in over the phone for the last stretch.

The pin shows Bwizibwera. An exact pin will be set once the farm's Google Business Profile is verified.

Planning a visit

  • Let us know at least a day ahead so someone is free to show you round
  • Weekdays work best — the farm is fully staffed and working
  • Wear boots or shoes you do not mind getting muddy
  • Groups of any size are welcome, but tell us the number in advance
  • A full walk of the blocks takes around two hours

What there is to see

The banana plantation and mother garden, the macro-propagation nursery, the coffee and mango blocks, the horticulture beds, the apiary, and — by arrangement — the livestock unit.

For organisations

Partnerships & programme delivery.

NGOs, government agencies, universities and vocational institutes: IBO Mixed Farm Ltd is an accredited delivery partner with a working demonstration site and twenty years of doing this.

We have delivered skilling programmes with AVSI under the SKY project, worked alongside NAADS, NARO and district production departments, and hosted student cohorts from institutions across Uganda.

  • UVTAB-accredited centre, DIT Centre No. MAC/1269
  • Capacity for up to 152 trainees per intake
  • Demonstration and production units on the same site
  • Attendance, assessment and completion records for reporting
  • Registered private limited company with a TIN, able to contract
