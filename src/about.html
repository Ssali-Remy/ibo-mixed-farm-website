Twenty years of getting more out of the same land.

IBO Mixed Farm Ltd is a family-owned commercial farm in Bwizibwera, Mbarara, and an accredited skilling centre that has trained over three thousand farmers and students.

Our story

It began with a question about land.

In 2004 the Arinaitwe family began farming here with a conviction that has since become the company motto: optimum utilization of land. Not one crop on all of it, but the right enterprise on each part of it, each one feeding the next.

Bananas went in on the deepest soils. Coffee and mangoes took the slopes. Vegetables filled the gaps between seasons. Cattle produced milk and, just as importantly, manure. Bees pollinated the orchard and produced honey on a fifth of an acre. Nothing was left idle.

Neighbours noticed. They came to ask how the banana mats stayed clean, how the herd stayed productive through the dry season, how a farm this size could carry so many enterprises without any of them failing. Answering those questions turned out to be a second business.

Today the farm is a registered private limited company and an accredited skilling centre under the Uganda Vocational and Technical Assessment Board — while remaining, in every practical sense, a working farm that has to sell what it grows.

Milestones

How the farm grew.

Two decades, told in the moments that changed what this place could do.

  • 2004

    The farm is established as a private mixed farm in Bwizibwera, Rwanyamahembe sub-county, built around the principle of optimum utilization of land.

  • Growth years

    The banana block expands to 9.5 acres and 4,222 plants. Coffee, the mango orchard, horticulture and the apiary are added alongside it.

  • The nursery

    A commercial banana macro-propagation nursery is built, with capacity for 100,000 plantlets — turning the farm into a source of clean planting material for the whole region.

  • Becoming a school

    Agricultural colleges, universities and government programmes begin sending students for industrial training and field attachment.

  • The SKY project

    With AVSI, IBO delivers skilling in agripreneurship for increased youth employment — training crop managers and plant doctors drawn from institutions across the country.

  • Accreditation

    IBO Mixed Farm Ltd is registered as a private limited company and accredited by UVTAB as a skilling centre, DIT Centre No. MAC/1269.

  • Today

    Over 3,000 farmers and students trained, roughly 80 farms helped into existence, and visitors received from eight countries.

Why IBO

Everything under one roof, on one farm.

Most places do one of these things. We do all of them, on the same fifteen acres, which is why a visit here answers more questions than a classroom can.

A working commercial farm

We are not a demonstration plot funded to look good. The bananas, milk, honey and vegetables are sold. If a method does not pay, we find out before you do.

Accredited and assessed

A UVTAB-accredited skilling centre with a DIT centre number, so the certificate a trainee leaves with is recognised beyond our gate.

Diagnosis, not guesswork

Soil testing and plant disease diagnosis on site. We can tell you what is actually wrong with your field instead of recommending inputs and hoping.

Crops and livestock together

Bananas, coffee, orchard, horticulture, dairy, small ruminants and bees — trainees see how the enterprises actually feed each other.

Affordable and short

Courses run from five to thirty days, mostly practical, at a price a smallholder or a student group can meet.

We stay in touch

Follow-up support after training, and an alumni network of farmers who trained here and are still farming.

How the farm is run

A three-member Board of Directors — with backgrounds in enterprise management, finance, economics and agriculture — provides governance. Day to day, the structure is deliberately flat:

  • BoardThree directors, setting direction and oversight
  • DirectorOverall leadership and partnerships
  • Farm ManagerDay-to-day operations across all blocks
  • Finance & AdminAccounts, records and trainee administration
  • ForemanSupervision of casual and seasonal labour

Our teams
