Service 01

Banana plantlets from our macro-propagation nursery.

A commercial nursery with capacity for 100,000 plantlets, producing clean, uniform planting material for new plantations and for farmers replacing tired mats.

The sucker dug out of a neighbour's garden is the cheapest planting material available and often the most expensive decision a banana farmer makes — it carries whatever that garden was carrying. Macro-propagated plantlets start clean, establish evenly, and let you plan a harvest instead of hoping for one.

  • Clean, disease-free starting material
  • Uniform plants, so the block matures together
  • Quantities from a smallholder plot to a full plantation
  • Guidance on spacing, holing and establishment included
  • Variety advice — including CWD-resistant material from our mother garden

Service 02

Soil testing.

Find out what your soil actually has before you spend money on inputs it does not need.

Most fertiliser in Uganda is bought on advice, habit or whatever the shop had in stock. A soil test replaces that with a number. We guide you on how to sample your field properly, run the test, and then sit down and explain what the results mean in terms of what to buy and what to skip.

  • Sampling guidance so the result reflects your whole field
  • Testing of the key parameters for your crop
  • A plain-language interpretation, not just a printout
  • Input and amendment recommendations tied to your budget

Service 03

Farm layout design & consultancy.

Where things go on a farm decides how much work it takes to run for the next twenty years.

We help you plan a new farm or reorganise an existing one — which enterprise belongs on which soil, how blocks are sized and spaced, where water and access roads run, where the shed and the store sit relative to everything else. It is the least glamorous service we offer and the one that saves the most money.

  • Site assessment and enterprise mix for your land
  • Block layout, spacing and rotation planning
  • Water, access and infrastructure siting
  • Phasing, so you build it as the money comes in
  • Pairs naturally with a soil test
Talk to us about your land
Service 04

Plant clinic & disease diagnosis.

Bring us a sick plant. We will tell you what is wrong with it and what to do about it.

Wrong diagnosis is expensive twice over: once for the chemical that was never going to work, and again for the crop you lose while you try it. Our plant clinic identifies the actual problem — disease, pest, deficiency or management — and gives you a course of action.

  • Field or bench diagnosis of crop problems
  • Practical control and management recommendations
  • Advice on preventing a repeat next season
  • Training of plant doctors for farmer groups and institutions
