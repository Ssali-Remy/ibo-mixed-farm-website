Our approach

Mostly boots, some blackboard.

Every course is built around doing the work, on the block where the work actually happens. Theory is kept to what you need in order to understand what your hands are doing.

You will cut corms in the nursery, mix media, hang hive frames, take soil samples, walk a field diagnosing disease, and sit in on the milking. The farm is not paused for trainees — you join it while it runs.

  • Small ratio of theory to practical, deliberately
  • Taught on a commercial farm, not a display plot
  • Trainers who farm for a living, plus credible external facilitators
  • Assessment and certification through an accredited centre
  • Follow-up support and an alumni network after you leave

Programmes

What you can come and learn.

Courses can be taken on their own or combined into a longer programme. Tell us what your group needs and we will shape the content around it.

Flagship

Banana macro-propagation

The technology that built this farm's nursery. Corm selection and preparation, sprouting chambers, media, weaning, hardening off and nursery hygiene — through to a plantlet you can sell or plant.

  • Selecting and preparing mother material
  • Building and managing a sprouting chamber
  • Weaning, hardening and quality control
  • Costing and running a nursery as a business

Livestock

Dairy farming operations

Run on a commercial cross-dairy herd. Feeding and herd health, milking hygiene, breeding decisions and the records that tell you whether the cow is paying for herself.

  • Feeding and dry-season strategy
  • Milking hygiene and handling
  • Herd health and routine management
  • Record-keeping and margins per animal

Livestock

Pasture establishment & management

Delivered as part of the SKY skilling programme. Species selection, establishment, rotation and conservation so the herd eats well through the dry months.

Plant health

Plant doctor & plant clinic operation

Learn to diagnose crop disease in the field, run a plant clinic session, and give other farmers advice you can stand behind. One of the two skilling tracks delivered here under the AVSI SKY project.

Crops

Crop manager

The full production cycle for the farm's main crops — establishment, agronomy, pest and disease management, harvesting and post-harvest handling — aimed at people who will manage a block or a farm.

Planning

Soil testing & farm layout

How to sample soil properly, what the results mean, and how to lay a farm out around them — blocks, spacing, water, access and enterprise mix.

Agribusiness

Value addition

Turning raw produce into something worth more — handling, processing, packaging and finding the buyer who pays the difference.

Soil health

Compost making

Building compost from what the farm already produces, and using it to cut the fertiliser bill without cutting yield.

The practical details

What it costs and how it runs.

Everything happens on the farm at Bwizibwera. There are no fixed term dates — we open a course when a group is ready, which means you can train when it suits your season rather than ours.

  • DurationFrom 5 days up to 30 days, depending on the programme
  • FormatMajority practical, with supporting theory
  • WhereOn the farm, Bwizibwera, Mbarara
  • Intake sizeUp to 152 trainees per intake
  • CalendarOn demand — you propose the dates
  • FeeUGX 400,000 per month, inclusive
  • CertificateIssued on completion; centre accredited by UVTAB
  • LanguageEnglish, with Runyankole available on request

The monthly fee is quoted as all-inclusive. Confirm exactly what is covered for your group — meals, accommodation, materials and certification — when you book, as this varies with course length and group size.

Certification

IBO Mixed Farm Ltd is an accredited skilling centre under the Uganda Vocational and Technical Assessment Board, DIT Centre No. MAC/1269. Trainees receive a certificate on completion.

After the course

Training does not end at the gate. We offer follow-up support and keep an alumni community of farmers who trained here — many of whom are now supplying their own districts.

For institutions

Placing a cohort with us.

We regularly host industrial-training and internship groups from universities, agricultural colleges and vocational institutes across Uganda, and deliver skilling programmes on behalf of NGOs and government agencies.

  • Accredited centre with a DIT centre number for assessment
  • Capacity for large cohorts — up to 152 trainees per intake
  • A demonstration unit and production unit on the same site
  • Attendance, assessment and completion records for your reporting
  • Experience delivering donor-funded skilling programmes
